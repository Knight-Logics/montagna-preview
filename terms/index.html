This is a design preview

These terms cover your use of this Knight Logics preview of a website for Stephen Francis Montagna. They do not cover stephenfrancismontagna.com, YouTube, or a bookseller you reach from an edition link. If those terms and these terms ever seem to disagree, the other site’s terms govern what you do on that site. These terms govern what you do here.

The preview is offered so the pages, the reading order, and the flipbooks can be judged. It is not a bookstore. It is not an offer to sell a book. It is not a contract with the author for a signed copy, a reservation, or a place in line.

The cart does not sell anything

You may put a paperback or a hardback into the cart and you may fill in a name, an email address, and a shipping address. Doing so does not create an order the author must fill. Stripe is not connected. No card is charged. No shipping is purchased. The record stays in your browser, as the Privacy page explains. If you need a copy of a novel, use the edition link on that book’s page and complete the purchase with the seller, or use the author’s official site if he points you to a shop there.

Prices shown here are the paperback and hardback prices taken from the author’s IngramSpark listings for this preview. A seller can change a price, a discount, or a shipping charge without this page knowing. The price on the seller’s page at the moment you pay is the price that matters. A missing price on this preview means the listing was not ready to show. It does not mean the book is free.

The novels are fiction

The books are novels. Military operations, political crises, scientific discoveries, and historical scenes in them are told as fiction. A page on this preview is not a news report, not a manual, and not advice for anything dangerous the stories imagine. Character names and institutions appear because the published novels use them. Their presence on a page is not a statement of fact about a living person beyond what the novel itself is.

Synopses on this preview are editorial summaries of the published descriptions. They are not the novels. A flipbook, where one is published, is a portfolio of pages, not a promise that every sentence of the novel is reprinted in the browser.

IN THE LIBRARY

From these terms back into the library

The terms above are the limit on the cart, the fiction, and the copying. The library they refer to is all 21 novels. Prices and edition links live on each novel page, not in the cart’s confirmation. flipbooks is the set of images the copyright page tells you not to republish. screening room is the set of YouTube players the terms do not control once they load.

The searches this library can honestly meet are historical fantasy and the dual timeline, for Wind Trilogy; and military thriller, spy thriller, espionage, high stakes, action thriller, ensemble cast, and series continuation, for Scorpion Trilogy and the standalone operations. “Mystery thriller books” drew more search interest than sci-fi novels or “fantasy fiction trends” from June 2025 through June 2026, with a peak in October 2025, which is why a thriller reader may arrive here and should then be shown the right shelf. Domestic thriller interest climbed into February 2026, and romantasy remains a separate boom. Neither phrase is a description of these novels, so the pages do not wear those labels. Fantasy was the most written and most requested genre on QueryTracker as of 17 December 2025. Birth of Wind is this library’s fantasy. It is not a invitation to relabel the Iran operations as fantasy because the larger genre is crowded.

Privacy explains the browser storage. Copyright explains the author’s copyright. Neither page turns the cart into a purchase.

Five further novels sit one link away from this page. Each note below is the published situation of that book, not a new plot. Follow the one that matches the search you actually typed.

Operation Dragon Claw puts a terrorist group on La Palma. They intend to place explosives along a crack in the Cumbre Vieja volcano. In the novel’s scenario, the collapse would throw an enormous mass of earth into the Atlantic and raise a tsunami. Dragon Fire troops are sent to stop the attack before the consequences leave the island. The published description is careful to call that collapse the novel’s scenario. This preview repeats that caution. It is fiction about a plan, not a forecast. Continue on Operation Dragon Claw.

Trump’s Brass Escalator continues Sandstorm. The president, the vice-president, Special Forces leader Walker, and his female sergeant are in New York to present evidence gathered in Iran to the United Nations. Before they can, robbers attack Trump Tower and take the vice-president hostage. Walker’s team has to break off the larger mission to stop the robbery and save a life at the center of government. Continue on Trump’s Brass Escalator.

The Scorpion Sting begins after Eagle’s Talon. Saddam Hussein sends another group into the United States to kill the Iraqi operative American forces captured, and to kill soldiers involved in that arrest. Walker’s team has to find the new cell. The danger follows the soldiers home. The published description is a retaliation story, which is why the series page treats it as the continuation rather than as a second first chapter. Continue on The Scorpion Sting.

Birth of Wind opens the Wind Trilogy in Japan in 1333. The published description sets a female samurai against a society that will not make a place for her, and it ends with a curse the shogun lays on her sword, binding the warrior and the blade. That is the spine of the novel on this site. The page does not pretend to replace the book, and it does not add battles, names, or a last chapter the published description does not support. Continue on Birth of Wind.

The Paradise of the Devil begins with a Cuban colonel who has lost faith in the condition of the military. He confronts Castro, kills him, and takes command of the island, expecting the people to support the takeover. A civilian army rises against his troops instead. Under that civil war sits a secret left from the Cuban Missile Crisis, and the secret pulls the United States into a confrontation that does not stay in Cuba. Continue on The Paradise of the Devil.

Those five are a path, not the whole library. All 21 novels remain listed together, the two trilogies stay in order on Series, and the twelve portfolios stay on Flipbooks.
