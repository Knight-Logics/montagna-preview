# Contact Stephen Francis Montagna — Readers, Press & Events

> How to reach novelist Stephen Francis Montagna with reader questions, bookshop and event enquiries, press requests and rights or permission requests.

Contact author Stephen Francis Montagna: reader questions, bookshop and event enquiries, press and interview requests, and permission or rights enquiries.

## What to include in your message

- Readers: the book you are asking about, and where you are in it
- Bookshops, libraries and book clubs: the place, the date you have in mind and the titles
- Press: the outlet, the format and your deadline
- Permissions: the passage, cover or trailer you would like to use, and how—see [copyright and permissions](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/copyright/)

## Questions and answers

### How do I ask permission to quote a Stephen Francis Montagna book?

Send the passage and how you would like to use it through this page. The [copyright page](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/copyright/) explains what needs permission.
