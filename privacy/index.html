07 / PRIVACY

Privacy policyNothing leaves
this browser.

What this website keeps, what it never collects, and what happens when you play a trailer or buy a book on IngramSpark.

Who runs this website

This is the official website of the novelist Stephen Francis Montagna. It was designed and built for him by Knight Logics. This policy covers this website only. It does not cover YouTube, the booksellers a book page links to, or any other site.

What this website does not collect

There is no account, no password and no checkout. Optional visitor analytics are described below. Your reading list stays in your own browser; this website does not send the saved list to the author or Knight Logics.

Optional visitor analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” this website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and Microsoft Clarity to understand page use through heatmaps and masked session recordings. The enabled services receive information about pages viewed, clicks, browser and device details, approximate location and cookies or similar identifiers. This website does not send them your reading list or messages, or use their advertising features.

Analytics services do not load before you allow them. Declining does not limit access to the website. Use the “Analytics choices” button to change your choice; withdrawing permission reloads the page with analytics off. Your choice is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. Browser privacy signals are respected and keep optional analytics off.

For more information, read Google’s privacy policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement. To ask about information collected through this website, email contact@stephenfmontagnabooks.com.

Searching the library loads a catalog file that is part of the website. That request asks only for the list of titles.

The reading list

The reading list is kept in your browser’s local storage, for this website only. The plus on a book adds it; the same browser shows the list again on your next visit. You can clear it with the button below, or by clearing this site’s stored data in your browser.

Buying a book

Stephen sells his books through IngramSpark. The paperback and hardback buttons on each book page open that edition’s page on IngramSpark’s store (shop.ingramspark.com) in a new tab. Your order, payment, shipping address and receipt are handled entirely by IngramSpark, under its own privacy policy and terms of sale. This website never sees them and takes no payment.

The contact form

Email sent to contact@stephenfmontagnabooks.com is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the inbox where the author’s correspondence is handled. The contact form does not send anything itself: it opens your own email app with your message addressed to that address, and you send it from there. Your message, name and email address are used only to reply. This website does not publish a telephone number.

Trailers and book portfolios

The book trailers are published on YouTube. On the trailers page they sit in a privacy-enhanced player from youtube-nocookie.com; on other pages the player loads only when you choose to play a trailer. Once it loads, YouTube handles the request under its own privacy policy.

The book portfolios are page images stored on this website. Turning their pages does not send you to another service or open an account anywhere.

Motion, younger readers and changes

The pause control in the header stops the background motion, and the site follows your device’s reduced-motion setting. A choice you make on the page itself is kept in local storage for this site.

The novels are fiction for adults, with war, terrorism and, in some books, biological or nuclear danger. This website is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

If the website changes the information it collects or how it is used, this policy will be updated. This version is dated 7 November 2026. See also the terms of use and copyright and permissions.
