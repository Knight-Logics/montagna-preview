07 / PRIVACY & THIS PREVIEW

Made for a conversation.
Kept under your control.

A private author website concept prepared by Knight Logics.

About the preview

This site is a design demonstration. It is not the author’s live website and is not connected to a checkout, mailing service, or contact backend. No production domain has been changed.

Your reading list

Saved books use local storage on your device. No account is created and the list is not sent to a server. You can clear it below.

Enquiry drafts

The contact form creates a text file locally. Form details are not stored in local storage or sent anywhere.

Background motion

Use the pause control in the navigation to stop or resume the animated scenery. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected by default. The page-turn reader also offers a continuous text view.

Trailers & external links

Video players connect to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed domain only after you choose to load a trailer. The Original document option loads an existing Heyzine flipbook inside this site only after you choose to load it. The newly typeset page-turn reader works with local assets. YouTube may then process information according to its own policies. Retailer and legacy website links are labelled and may open in a new tab.

Author materials

Book covers, author photography, synopses, and quotations come from Stephen Francis Montagna’s published website. Original source material has been retained for review. Atmospheric background artwork was created for this concept and is illustrative.

Search visibility

This local preview includes noindex directives and a robots.txt disallow rule. It has not been publicly deployed. A future hosted demo should also require authentication; crawler directives alone do not provide access control.
