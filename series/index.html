READER’S GUIDE

Reading order: two trilogies and the thrillers that share their characters

Reading order for Stephen Francis Montagna’s two trilogies and the thrillers that share characters: Robert Walker, the Dragon Fire troops, and the Sandstorm sequel.

The Wind Trilogy, in order

  1. Birth of Wind — Japan, 1333: the female samurai and the shogun’s curse on her sword
  2. The Wind’s Breathe — Japan, 1996: the sword is found and put to work
  3. Night Wind — the United States: an FBI agent brings the blade home

The Scorpion Trilogy, in order

  1. Eagle’s Talon — book one: the plot against the President and the capture mission in Iraq
  2. The Scorpion Sting — continues where Eagle’s Talon ends: retaliation inside the United States
  3. Daughter of the Scorpion — completes the trilogy: a designer bioweapon in the Iraqi desert

Robert Walker beyond the trilogy

Robert Walker recurs far beyond the Scorpion books. The novels’ own portfolios and descriptions name him in The Paradise of the Devil (as a corporal), Bio-Level Three, Sandstorm and Trump’s Brass Escalator (as a lieutenant), and The Vulture Will Lead You to Death and God’s Sword of Justice (as a captain). The author has not published a single chronology for those books; each stands on its own.

Other connected novels

  • Sandstorm, then Trump’s Brass Escalator: the author describes the second as the continuation of the first.
  • The Dragon Fire troops: Operation Dragon Fire, Bio-Level Three and Operation Dragon Claw. No numbered order has been published.

Questions about the reading order

What order should I read the Wind Trilogy in?

Birth of Wind, then The Wind’s Breathe, then Night Wind.

What order should I read the Scorpion Trilogy in?

Eagle’s Talon first; The Scorpion Sting continues where it ends; Daughter of the Scorpion completes the trilogy.

Which Stephen Francis Montagna books feature Robert Walker?

Nine: the three Scorpion novels plus The Paradise of the Devil, Bio-Level Three, Sandstorm, Trump’s Brass Escalator, The Vulture Will Lead You to Death and God’s Sword of Justice.

Is Trump’s Brass Escalator a sequel to Sandstorm?

Yes. The author describes Trump’s Brass Escalator as the continuation of Sandstorm.
