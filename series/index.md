# Stephen Francis Montagna Books in Order: Wind & Scorpion

> Reading order for Stephen Francis Montagna’s two trilogies and the thrillers that share characters: Robert Walker, the Dragon Fire troops, and the Sandstorm sequel.

Canonical: https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/series/  
Author: Stephen Francis Montagna  
Updated: 2026-11-07

Stephen Francis Montagna books in order: both trilogies, the Sandstorm sequel, and every novel featuring Robert Walker or the Dragon Fire troops.

## Reading order: two trilogies and the thrillers that share their characters

### The Wind Trilogy, in order

1. [Birth of Wind](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/birth-of-wind/) — Japan, 1333: the female samurai and the shogun’s curse on her sword
2. [The Wind’s Breathe](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-wind-s-breathe/) — Japan, 1996: the sword is found and put to work
3. [Night Wind](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/night-wind/) — the United States: an FBI agent brings the blade home

### The Scorpion Trilogy, in order

1. [Eagle’s Talon](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/eagle-s-talon/) — book one: the plot against the President and the capture mission in Iraq
2. [The Scorpion Sting](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-scorpion-sting/) — continues where Eagle’s Talon ends: retaliation inside the United States
3. [Daughter of the Scorpion](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/daughter-of-the-scorpion/) — completes the trilogy: a designer bioweapon in the Iraqi desert

### Robert Walker beyond the trilogy

Robert Walker recurs far beyond the Scorpion books. The novels’ own portfolios and descriptions name him in [The Paradise of the Devil](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/) (as a corporal), [Bio-Level Three](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/bio-level-three/), [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/) and [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/) (as a lieutenant), and [The Vulture Will Lead You to Death](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-vulture-will-lead-you-to-death/) and [God’s Sword of Justice](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/god-s-sword-of-justice/) (as a captain). The author has not published a single chronology for those books; each stands on its own.

### Other connected novels

- [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/), then [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/): the author describes the second as the continuation of the first.
- The Dragon Fire troops: [Operation Dragon Fire](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/operation-dragon-fire/), [Bio-Level Three](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/bio-level-three/) and [Operation Dragon Claw](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/operation-dragon-claw/). No numbered order has been published.

## Questions and answers

### What order should I read the Wind Trilogy in?

[Birth of Wind](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/birth-of-wind/), then [The Wind’s Breathe](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-wind-s-breathe/), then [Night Wind](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/night-wind/).

### What order should I read the Scorpion Trilogy in?

[Eagle’s Talon](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/eagle-s-talon/) first; [The Scorpion Sting](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-scorpion-sting/) continues where it ends; [Daughter of the Scorpion](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/daughter-of-the-scorpion/) completes the trilogy.

### Which Stephen Francis Montagna books feature Robert Walker?

Nine: the three Scorpion novels plus [The Paradise of the Devil](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/), [Bio-Level Three](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/bio-level-three/), [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/), [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/), [The Vulture Will Lead You to Death](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-vulture-will-lead-you-to-death/) and [God’s Sword of Justice](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/god-s-sword-of-justice/).

### Is Trump’s Brass Escalator a sequel to Sandstorm?

Yes. The author describes [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/) as the continuation of [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/).
