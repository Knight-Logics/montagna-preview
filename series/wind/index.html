Begin with Birth of Wind ↗

The Wind Trilogy moves through three settings, linked by a warrior’s life and the curse that binds her to a sword. Reading in sequence lets the meaning of that bond unfold before the blade reaches each new owner.

Begin with the female samurai in Japan in 1333. The second novel moves to Japan in 1996, where the rediscovered sword carries Wind’s abilities into a new kind of conflict. Night Wind follows the blade to the United States with an FBI agent, extending the warrior’s obligation across the Pacific.

THE READING ORDER

The Wind Trilogy

A female samurai. A cursed sword. Three lives across time.

01
BOOK 1

Birth of Wind

In Japan in 1333, a female samurai fights for her place in a society dominated by men. Her discipline, determination, and skill carry her through a life of conflict. When her story reaches its end, a curse placed on her sword by the shogun binds the warrior and the blade together for eternity. Birth of Wind begins a trilogy that carries her legacy across centuries.

↗02
BOOK 2

The Wind’s Breathe

Centuries after the events of Birth of Wind, the warrior’s cursed sword is discovered in Japan in 1996. Its new owner uses Wind’s samurai abilities against rivals in the construction industry. The second Wind novel moves the story from the battlefields of the fourteenth century into a modern world where the sword’s power finds a very different purpose.

↗03
BOOK 3

Night Wind

Wind’s sword is discovered once again, this time by the FBI. The agent responsible for it brings the blade back to the United States and soon learns that it is far more than an ordinary weapon. Wind remains bound to protect the person who possesses it, bringing the warrior’s enduring obligation into the agent’s life. The third novel carries the Wind Trilogy across the Pacific into a new setting.
