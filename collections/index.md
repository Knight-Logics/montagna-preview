Stephen Francis Montagna’s books on three shelves: seven new releases, the 2026 collection of military thrillers, and the 2025 collection with both trilogies.

## Three shelves, twenty-one novels

- [New Release](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/new-release/) — seven books: The Paradise of the Devil, The Vulture Will Lead You to Death, The Vector, The Warning of Ramses, IFF: Identify Friend or Foe, God’s Sword of Justice and Operation Glass Slipper
- [2026 collection](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/collections/2026/) — five books: Operation Dragon Fire, Bio-Level Three, Operation Dragon Claw, Beyond Yesterday and Code Cold Fire Alert
- [2025 collection](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/collections/2025/) — nine books: both trilogies, Trump’s Brass Escalator, Sandstorm and Massive Vinegar Joe Platform

## Questions and answers

### What are Stephen Francis Montagna’s newest books?

The seven on the [New Release shelf](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/new-release/), led by [The Paradise of the Devil](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/) and [The Vector](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/the-vector/).
