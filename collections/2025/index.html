FIRST IN THIS GROUP

Trump’s Brass Escalator

A hostage crisis interrupts a mission to expose the truth.

Continuing the story of Sandstorm, this novel brings the President, Vice-President, Special Forces leader Walker, and his female sergeant to New York. They are preparing to present evidence gathered in Iran to the United Nations. Before they can do so, robbers attack Trump Tower and take the Vice-President hostage. Walker and his team must interrupt their larger mission to stop the robbery and save a life at the center of government.

READER’S GUIDE

Both trilogies, a two-book mission and a war in the Pacific

The 2025 collection holds nine Stephen Francis Montagna novels: the Wind and Scorpion trilogies, Sandstorm, its sequel Trump’s Brass Escalator, and Massive Vinegar Joe Platform.

Six of the nine books are the two trilogies: the female-samurai Wind Trilogy and Robert Walker’s Scorpion Trilogy. Sandstorm and Trump’s Brass Escalator read as a pair—the evidence carried out of Iran in the first is what brings Walker to Trump Tower in the second. Massive Vinegar Joe Platform turns to the Pacific, where China moves on North Korea, Hong Kong and Taiwan.

Questions about the 2025 collection

Which 2025 collection books should be read in order?

The two trilogies, and Sandstorm before Trump’s Brass Escalator.
