# 2025 Collection: Wind & Scorpion Trilogies | Montagna

The 2025 collection: the Wind and Scorpion trilogies plus Sandstorm, Trump’s Brass Escalator and Massive Vinegar Joe Platform. Nine novels in all.

## Both trilogies, a two-book mission and a war in the Pacific

Six of the nine books are the two trilogies: the female-samurai [Wind Trilogy](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/series/wind/) and Robert Walker’s [Scorpion Trilogy](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/series/scorpion/). [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/) and [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/) read as a pair—the evidence carried out of Iran in the first is what brings Walker to Trump Tower in the second. [Massive Vinegar Joe Platform](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/massive-vinegar-joe-platform/) turns to the Pacific, where China moves on North Korea, Hong Kong and Taiwan.

## Questions and answers

### Which 2025 collection books should be read in order?

The two trilogies, and [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/) before [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/).
