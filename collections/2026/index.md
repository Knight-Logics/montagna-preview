The 2026 collection: Operation Dragon Fire, Bio-Level Three, Operation Dragon Claw, Code Cold Fire Alert and the time travel novel Beyond Yesterday.

## Dragon Fire missions, a weapons plant and a raid on the past

Three of the five books follow the same specialized unit, the Dragon Fire troops: a dirty-bomb plot in Washington in [Operation Dragon Fire](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/operation-dragon-fire/), weapons factories in the Libyan desert in [Bio-Level Three](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/bio-level-three/) and a volcano rigged to raise a mega-tsunami in [Operation Dragon Claw](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/operation-dragon-claw/). [Code Cold Fire Alert](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/code-cold-fire-alert/) moves inside a plant built to destroy America’s own chemical and biological weapons, and [Beyond Yesterday](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/beyond-yesterday/) leaves the present altogether for a post-nuclear future that steals people from the past.
