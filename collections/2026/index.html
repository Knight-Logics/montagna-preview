READER’S GUIDE

Dragon Fire missions, a weapons plant and a raid on the past

The 2026 collection gathers five Stephen Francis Montagna novels: the three Dragon Fire troops thrillers, Code Cold Fire Alert and the time-travel novel Beyond Yesterday.

Three of the five books follow the same specialized unit, the Dragon Fire troops: a dirty-bomb plot in Washington in Operation Dragon Fire, weapons factories in the Libyan desert in Bio-Level Three and a volcano rigged to raise a mega-tsunami in Operation Dragon Claw. Code Cold Fire Alert moves inside a plant built to destroy America’s own chemical and biological weapons, and Beyond Yesterday leaves the present altogether for a post-nuclear future that steals people from the past.

Questions about the 2026 collection

Which 2026 collection books feature the Dragon Fire troops?

Operation Dragon Fire, Bio-Level Three and Operation Dragon Claw.
