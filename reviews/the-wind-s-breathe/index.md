## The review

Reviewer: Alexandra Pierce  
Outlet: Pacific Book Review

In The Winds Breathe, the second installment of Stephen Francis Montagna’s Wind Trilogy, the author expands upon the sweeping mythological and historical foundation established in The Birth of Wind, delivering a narrative that is both more intense and more refined in its storytelling. This continuation deepens the saga’s exploration of power, destiny, and the enduring influence of both divine forces and human ambition.

Montagna demonstrates a notable evolution in narrative control, guiding readers through a world where the consequences of earlier conflicts begin to fully unfold. The novel builds upon its predecessor’s fusion of mythology and historical realism, presenting a landscape shaped by war, loyalty, and the unseen forces that govern fate. The presence of elemental symbolism—particularly the recurring motif of wind—serves as both a literal and philosophical thread, representing change, destruction, and renewal.

One of the novel’s strengths lies in its heightened sense of momentum. While maintaining the rich historical context that defines the series, The Winds Breathe places greater emphasis on character movement and narrative progression. The stakes feel more immediate, and the conflicts more personal, allowing readers to connect more deeply with the individuals navigating this turbulent world. Montagna’s ability to balance large-scale events with moments of introspection adds depth and dimension to the story.

The author’s prose continues to reflect an epic, almost classical tone, reinforcing the grandeur of the narrative. His descriptive passages are vivid and deliberate, often immersing the reader in the atmosphere of feudal landscapes, battlefields, and shifting alliances. This stylistic consistency strengthens the trilogy’s identity, making it feel cohesive and purposeful.

However, as with the first installment, the novel’s density may challenge some readers. The intricate layering of historical detail, combined with a broad cast of characters, requires sustained attention. While this complexity enriches the story, it may occasionally slow the pacing for those seeking a more streamlined narrative experience.

Despite these challenges, The Winds Breathe succeeds in advancing the trilogy with confidence and clarity. It not only builds upon the foundation of the first book but also elevates the stakes, setting the stage for a powerful conclusion. Montagna’s commitment to crafting a story that is both intellectually engaging and narratively ambitious remains evident throughout.

### Pacific Book Review Verdict:

The Winds Breathe is a compelling and sophisticated continuation of the Wind Trilogy, offering a richer, more dynamic narrative that will resonate with readers who appreciate epic fiction grounded in history, mythology, and the forces that shape human destiny.
