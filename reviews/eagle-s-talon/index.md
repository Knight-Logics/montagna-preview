## The review

Reviewer: Caroline M. Reyes, Senior Resident Reviewer  
Outlet: Author’s Choice LLC

Stephen Francis Montagna’s Eagle’s Talon is a high-intensity geopolitical thriller that immerses readers in the volatile aftermath of the Gulf War, weaving together themes of revenge, ideological extremism, and global power struggles. From its opening prologue, the novel establishes a stark and uncompromising tone, grounding the narrative in the historical realities of Operation Desert Storm and its lingering consequences across the Middle East.

Montagna demonstrates a commanding grasp of military and political context, using real-world tensions as the backbone of a fictional yet believable scenario. The early chapters introduce a dangerous terrorist cell operating within Washington, D.C., driven by anger, loss, and a desire to retaliate against the United States. Their internal debates over potential targets—including high-ranking government officials and symbolic institutions—create a sustained sense of urgency and unpredictability, immediately pulling the reader into a web of impending crisis.

One of the novel’s strongest elements lies in its character dynamics. The interactions between Captain Jebril Briegheeth, the calculating yet conflicted leader, .Farseeha al-Mana, the fiercely ideological and relentless operative, and Mamdouh al-Qassan, the younger and more pragmatic participant, form a compelling psychological triangle. Their clashing perspectives on strategy, loyalty, and sacrifice add depth to the narrative, elevating it beyond a conventional action-driven plot. The tension within the group mirrors the broader instability of the geopolitical landscape they inhabit.

Montagna’s writing is unapologetically direct and descriptive, particularly in his portrayal of military history and ideological motivations. The novel does not shy away from depicting the harsh realities of war, nor does it dilute the intensity of extremist viewpoints, which are presented in a raw and unfiltered manner. This approach lends authenticity to the story, though it also demands careful engagement from the reader due to its intensity and complexity.

The pacing is deliberate, especially in the early chapters, where detailed planning and surveillance sequences build a slow-burning suspense. As the narrative progresses, this groundwork promises to evolve into a gripping and explosive confrontation. The author’s attention to procedural detail— whether in military operations or covert planning—enhances the realism and stakes of the story.

Recommendation: Eagle’s Talon is highly recommended for readers who appreciate:

- Military and political thrillers grounded in real-world events

- Complex antagonists with layered motivations

- Detailed, strategy-driven narratives

- Stories exploring the long-term consequences of war and conflict

### Conclusion

A bold and unflinching thriller, Eagle’s Talon showcases Stephen Francis Montagna’s strength in blending historical context with fictional suspense. With its intense character interplay and high-stakes narrative, the novel stands as a gripping entry in the realm of military fiction, appealing to readers seeking both action and depth.
