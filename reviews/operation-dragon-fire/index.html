Operation Dragon Fire: the review in full

Reviewed by Santi Singh · AuthorsChoice LLC

Stephen Francis Montagna’s Operation Dragon Fire is a gripping military thriller that delivers nonstop suspense, high-stakes action, and a chilling look at modern terrorism. Written with intensity and patriotic conviction, this novel plunges readers into a terrifying scenario where a carefully orchestrated series of attacks threatens the heart of the United States.

The story follows an Iraqi terrorist who gains entry into America under the guise of assisting U.S. troops overseas, only to secretly build a network of radicalized young operatives. What unfolds is a coordinated plan involving multiple devastating strikes: attacks on New York’s subway system, Kennedy International Airport, Washington, D.C., and ultimately the detonation of a dirty nuclear bomb near the White House and Capitol Building. As the nation faces chaos, the elite Dragon Fire troops are called into action to stop disaster before it is too late.

Montagna excels at creating urgency and tension, moving the narrative swiftly between political leadership, intelligence operations, and battlefield-style counterterrorism missions. The novel’s pacing is relentless, making it difficult to put down once the threat begins to unfold. Readers who enjoy tactical military fiction, covert operations, and large-scale suspense will find much to appreciate here.

One of the strongest aspects of Operation Dragon Fire is its portrayal of heroism under pressure. The Dragon Fire troops embody courage, sacrifice, and discipline as they race against time to protect millions of lives. Their mission adds emotional weight to the explosive storyline and gives readers characters to root for amid the danger.

Montagna also explores timely themes such as national security, trust, immigration vulnerabilities, and the complexity of combating hidden threats within modern society. These elements add realism and relevance, making the story feel unsettlingly plausible.

Conclusion

Operation Dragon Fire is a bold, action-packed thriller that combines military strategy, suspense, and patriotic resolve. Stephen Francis Montagna delivers a dramatic page-turner that will appeal to fans of Tom Clancy-style fiction and readers who enjoy stories where elite forces stand between civilization and catastrophe. A tense and entertaining read from start to finish.
