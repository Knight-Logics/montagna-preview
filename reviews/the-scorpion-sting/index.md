## The review

Reviewer: Daniel P. Whitaker, Resident Reviewer  
Outlet: Author’s Choice LLC

Stephen Francis Montagna’s The Scorpion’s Sting is a gripping and uncompromising geopolitical thriller that plunges readers into a world of covert operations, ideological extremism, and high-stakes international conflict. From its opening prologue, the novel establishes a tense and volatile narrative centered on a terrorist cell operating within the United States, tasked with executing an attack designed to destabilize global power structures and shift political momentum.

Montagna demonstrates a strong command of tension and pacing early on, as internal disagreements among the operatives—ranging from targeting the American President to striking symbolic or strategic assets—create an atmosphere of unpredictability and danger. This layered conflict not only heightens suspense but also provides insight into the motivations and fractured dynamics within extremist groups.

As the narrative progresses, the story expands into a broader military and political framework, incorporating elite American special operations forces, intelligence agencies, and high-ranking officials. The attempted assassination sequence stands out as one of the novel’s most intense moments, combining precision, chaos, and consequence. The failure of the attack, coupled with its deadly aftermath—including the loss of key government figures—propels the story into a relentless pursuit of justice and retaliation.

Montagna’s strength lies in his attention to operational detail and realism. The depiction of military missions, particularly the covert extraction operation in Iraq, is vivid and immersive. Scenes of tactical movement, combat engagement, and soldier interaction are rendered with authenticity, giving readers a front-line perspective of modern warfare. The dialogue among soldiers, often raw and unfiltered, reinforces the intensity of the environment and adds a layer of gritty realism to the narrative.

At the same time, the novel explores deeper themes of loyalty, vengeance, and the moral ambiguity of war. Characters on both sides of the conflict are driven by conviction, ideology, and personal loss, creating a complex portrayal of a world where clear distinctions between right and wrong are often blurred.

While the novel’s scope and detail are impressive, its density may require careful attention from readers. The extensive cast of characters, combined with detailed military and political exposition, can occasionally slow the pacing. However, for readers who appreciate richly layered and realistic thrillers, this complexity enhances the overall experience.

Recommendation: The Scorpion’s Sting is highly recommended for readers who enjoy military thrillers, espionage fiction, and politically driven narratives. Fans of Tom Clancy-style storytelling will find this novel particularly engaging, as it delivers both strategic depth and action-packed sequences. This book is ideal for those who appreciate:

- Realistic portrayals of counterterrorism and special operations

- High-stakes political and international conflict

- Intense, suspense-driven storytelling

- Complex characters shaped by war and ideology

### Conclusion

A powerful and immersive opening to the Scorpion Trilogy, The Scorpion’s Sting delivers a compelling blend of action, realism, and geopolitical intrigue—making it a standout choice for fans of modern military fiction.
