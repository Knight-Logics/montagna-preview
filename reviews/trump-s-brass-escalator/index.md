### Novel Highlights

Trump’s Brass Escalator by Stephen Francis Montagna is an ambitious, high-octane political thriller that fuses military realism with speculative global conflict, delivering a narrative that feels both cinematic and provocatively timely. From its opening sequence inside the Pentagon— where high-ranking officials dissect the aftermath of a covert strike on Iranian nuclear facilities— the novel wastes no time immersing readers in a world of geopolitical tension, covert operations, and moral ambiguity.

Montagna demonstrates a clear strength in constructing large-scale scenarios. His depiction of multinational military operations, particularly the MNRRF (Multi-National Rapid Response Force), is rich in detail and intensity. The author’s attention to operational structure, chain of command, and battlefield logistics lends the story an almost documentary-like authenticity. Scenes aboard the aircraft carrier and within active combat zones are especially vivid—gritty, chaotic, and emotionally charged—capturing both the physical toll of war and the psychological strain on soldiers.

At the center of the novel lies a cast of hardened, deeply human characters. Figures like Lieutenant Robert Walker and Doctor Joel Russbinder provide contrasting lenses into the narrative—one shaped by relentless combat experience, the other by reluctant immersion into the machinery of war. Montagna excels at portraying camaraderie, grief, and resilience among elite soldiers, particularly in moments of loss and tension. These emotional undercurrents elevate the novel beyond action alone, giving weight to the consequences of every mission and decision.

Thematically, Trump’s Brass Escalator explores power, leadership, and the ethical gray zones of preemptive warfare. The political backdrop—featuring a commanding U.S. president navigating global threats—adds a layer of urgency and relevance. While the narrative occasionally leans heavily into exposition, particularly in technical briefings, these passages reinforce the complexity of the world Montagna has built rather than detract from it.

Where the novel stands out most is in its unapologetic intensity. Montagna does not shy away from depicting the brutality of modern warfare or the controversial tactics employed in its execution. This rawness may not suit every reader, but for fans of military thrillers, it offers a gripping, unfiltered experience.

### Recommendation

Trump’s Brass Escalator is a compelling read for those who appreciate detailed military fiction with a strong political edge. Readers of Tom Clancy-style narratives will find much to admire in Montagna’s scope, realism, and relentless pacing. It is a bold and immersive novel that challenges readers to confront the cost of global security in an increasingly unstable world.

### Conclusion

A powerful and expansive thriller that blends tactical precision with emotional depth—best suited for readers seeking a serious, thought-provoking take on modern warfare and international power dynamics.
