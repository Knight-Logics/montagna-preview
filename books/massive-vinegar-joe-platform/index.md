## A deception across the Pacific and a floating base built to answer it

Massive Vinegar Joe Platform is a Pacific military and political thriller. China uses a ploy to attack North Korea with the blessing of the United Nations, promising to eliminate the North’s nuclear weapons. The deception continues through a scheme over Hong Kong whose true purpose is to plunder its riches and to attack Taiwan.

The United States, with no military bases in the region in the novel’s scenario, is forced to connect a massive floating base—the platform of the title—for the safety of the world. The editorial review published with the novel, by Olivia Johnson for PacificReviews, calls it an ambitious, action-packed political thriller and praises its portrayal of leadership under pressure.
