A WORK OF FICTION

A rebel-led Libyan military command is secretly constructing twin chemical and biological factories deep in the desert. Once American intelligence discovers the project, the President orders specialized Dragon Fire troops to destroy the facilities before they become operational. The mission must eliminate the threat before the factories can place the wider world in danger.

Read the full published book description ＋

After the discovery of what the rebel lead Libyan military command was secretly constructing deep in their vast desert, the President of the United States immediately orders the Units of specialized Dragon Fire troops to be dispatched in order to destroy a set of twin Chemical and Biological factories before they became operational and a serious threat to the security of the rest of the world.

IN THE LIBRARY

A reader’s guide to Bio-Level Three

The story, the editions, and the reading order for Bio-Level Three are on this page. The guide below is here so the novel can be found by the searches that actually describe it, and so the next book is a link rather than a dead end.

Bio-Level Three sends specialized Dragon Fire troops into the Libyan desert. A rebel-led military command is secretly building twin chemical and biological factories. American intelligence finds the project. The president orders the factories destroyed before they can operate. The published description is a preemption story: stop the buildings before the weapons exist, not investigate a crime after a city is already sick.

Military thriller and high stakes match the order and the deadline. Espionage matches the intelligence that discovers the site. Biological thriller matches the payload. None of those phrases requires calling the book a medical procedural. The factories are the threat. Code Cold Fire Alert is the novel about plants built to destroy an existing American stockpile, which is the opposite engineering problem: demolition and containment, not a secret foreign construction project.

Read it beside Operation Dragon Fire and Operation Dragon Claw when you want the Dragon Fire unit across three missions. Read it beside Daughter of the Scorpion when you want two different deserts and two different biological programs.

Five further novels sit one link away from this page. Each note below is the published situation of that book, not a new plot. Follow the one that matches the search you actually typed.

Sandstorm sends American special operations soldiers into Iran. A rogue Russian admiral has sold Iran nuclear-tipped missiles launched from submarines. The team has to destroy the missiles and the submarines, and it has to bring back evidence of the transfer, evidence that could show how Iran acquired the means to build and launch a nuclear threat against the United States. The published description is a raid plus a proof. Continue on Sandstorm.

Daughter of the Scorpion turns on secret military papers that expose an Iraqi biological weapons program. American intelligence learns the pathogen could mutate and threaten life on a global scale if it is released. Captain Robert Walker leads a rapid response team into the Iraqi desert to find a young woman believed to know where the weapon is hidden. The clock in the published description is that search, not a courtroom drama and not a laboratory procedural. Continue on Daughter of the Scorpion.

The Wind’s Breathe is the second novel. Centuries after Birth of Wind, the cursed sword is found in Japan in 1996. Its new owner turns Wind’s samurai abilities against rivals in the construction industry. The published description is that specific: not a return to the fourteenth-century war, but the same binding put to work in a modern commercial fight. The title on the author’s materials uses this spelling, and so does this preview. Continue on The Wind’s Breathe.

The Vulture Will Lead You to Death follows terrorists who enter the United States from Cuba with a plan against the Port of Miami. They know a supertanker is docked there. They do not know that a ship carrying military supplies is stationed nearby, and that its presence could magnify the blast. The published description stops on that unawareness. The novel is the working out of an attack whose second weapon is the port itself. Continue on The Vulture Will Lead You to Death.

IFF: Identify Friend or Foe turns on a stolen aircraft. An Iranian pilot takes an F-18 and turns it against an American carrier in the Persian Gulf. The friendly identification signal, the system built to stop friendly fire, also stops the fleet from treating the aircraft as an enemy. A safeguard becomes the opening for the attack. The title is the premise, written out so a reader can see the trap before the novel springs it. Continue on IFF: Identify Friend or Foe.
