After the discovery of what the rebel lead Libyan military command was secretly constructing deep in their vast desert, the President of the United States immediately orders the Units of specialized Dragon Fire troops to be dispatched in order to destroy a set of twin Chemical and Biological factories before they became operational and a serious threat to the security of the rest of the world.

## Twin weapons factories in the Libyan desert and the order to destroy them

Bio-Level Three is a special-operations military thriller. Deep in the vast Libyan desert, a rebel-led military command is secretly building twin chemical and biological weapons factories. When the discovery reaches the President of the United States, he orders units of the specialized Dragon Fire troops to destroy both plants before they become operational and a threat to the rest of the world.

The book portfolio sets the story primarily in 1998 and follows Lieutenant Robert Walker and Sergeant Dorothy Ramirez, whose unit is stationed at Port Said, Egypt, close enough to Libya to launch the raid. President Albert Cole, Colonel Bruce Leadbetter, General John White and Doctor Kruse are among the others in the story. Its themes are duty and sacrifice, loyalty to the unit, weapons of mass destruction, and the gap between military and political decisions.

### When and where is Bio-Level Three set?

Primarily in 1998, according to the book portfolio, in the United States, Egypt, Libya and other international locations. Walker’s unit trains at Port Said before the mission into the Libyan desert.

### Is Bio-Level Three a standalone novel?

Yes. Bio-Level Three stands alone; no other book needs to be read first. It shares characters with other novels: Lieutenant Robert Walker also appears across the author’s other thrillers and leads the [Scorpion Trilogy](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/series/scorpion/); the Dragon Fire troops also carry [Operation Dragon Fire](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/operation-dragon-fire/) and [Operation Dragon Claw](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/operation-dragon-claw/).
