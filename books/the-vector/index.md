### The author’s published description

After the attack on Israel on October 7th, and with the United States joining the war. Iran decides to strike the United States. Iran gets eight volunteers, removes a tooth from each of the volunteers and replaces them with hollowed out teeth with a deadly biological agent stored in the false tooth. Then these Vectors, a Vector is someone infected with a disease and these Vectors are ordered to go to Mexico, enter the United States through the El-Passo Pass with orders to head for eight major cities. There they are to destroy the false tooth, giving the Vectors three to five days to infect ten X ten x ten people before the Vector's succumb to the deadly germ.

## Eight volunteers, eight cities and a weapon hidden in a tooth

The Vector is a geopolitical bioterrorism thriller set after the October 7 attack on Israel. With the United States joining the war, Iran decides to strike America directly. Eight volunteers each have a tooth removed and replaced with a hollow false tooth holding a deadly biological agent. They are sent to Mexico, across the border and on toward eight major American cities.

In the author’s words, a vector is someone infected with a disease. Once each volunteer is in place, the order is to destroy the false tooth and use the three to five days before the germ kills them to infect as many people as they can. The attack needs no missile and no bomb, only people. The editorial review published with the novel names Nasser Makarem Taleqani among the handlers who run the operation and recommends the book to readers of Tom Clancy, Vince Flynn and hyper-realistic international espionage.

### What is The Vector about?

### What does “vector” mean in The Vector?

A vector is a person infected with a disease who carries it to others. Each of Iran’s eight volunteers becomes one: a human carrier sent to spread an agent hidden in a false tooth.

### When is The Vector set?

In the aftermath of the October 7, 2023 attack on Israel, with the United States drawn into the war. The review published with the novel gives the date.

### Is The Vector a standalone novel?

Yes. The Vector stands alone; no other book needs to be read first.
