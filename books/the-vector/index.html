THE STORY

Eight operatives. Eight cities. A threat without borders.

A WORK OF FICTION

In this geopolitical thriller, Iran responds to war with a covert biological attack on the United States. Eight volunteers are fitted with false teeth containing a deadly agent and sent through Mexico toward eight American cities. Their orders are to release the agent and spread the infection before it kills them. The novel follows the threat of an attack carried by people rather than conventional weapons.

Read the full published book description ＋

After the attack on Israel on October 7th, and with the United States joining the war. Iran decides to strike the United States. Iran gets eight volunteers, removes a tooth from each of the volunteers and replaces them with hollowed out teeth with a deadly biological agent stored in the false tooth. Then these Vectors, a Vector is someone infected with a disease and these Vectors are ordered to go to Mexico, enter the United States through the El-Passo Pass with orders to head for eight major cities. There they are to destroy the false tooth, giving the Vectors three to five days to infect ten X ten x ten people before the Vector's succumb to the deadly germ.

A reader’s guide to The Vector

The story, the editions, and the reading order for The Vector are on this page. The guide below is here so the novel can be found by the searches that actually describe it, and so the next book is a link rather than a dead end.

The Vector is the preview’s plain example of a geopolitical thriller. Iran answers a war with a covert biological attack on the United States. Eight volunteers are fitted with false teeth that hold a deadly agent and are sent through Mexico toward eight American cities. Their orders are to release the agent and spread the infection before it kills them. The weapon in the published description is a person, not a missile.

Spy fiction had an unusually crowded publishing year in 2026, which is a critic’s observation rather than a claim that this novel appears on any best-of list. Five further novels sit one link away from this page. Each note below is the published situation of that book, not a new plot. Follow the one that matches the search you actually typed.

The Warning of Ramses starts from Egypt’s dependence on the Nile. Ethiopia builds a dam on the Blue Nile and sharply reduces the water reaching Egypt. The dispute moves toward war. Libya joins Egypt. The confrontation also involves the United States and the dam project. A river becomes a regional crisis. That is the published spine, and it is enough to tell you the novel is about water, alliances, and the point where a resource war stops being a negotiation. Continue on The Warning of Ramses.

Operation Glass Slipper begins with a scientific gift. A doctor finds a microbe that lives by consuming carbon in crude oil. With a microbiologist, he alters it so the organism can travel through the air and consume carbon pollution. The same discovery is then seen by a small group of terrorists as a path toward a biological weapon. The published description holds both uses in one research project: a repair for the atmosphere, and a weapon made from that repair. Continue on Operation Glass Slipper.

Operation Dragon Claw puts a terrorist group on La Palma. They intend to place explosives along a crack in the Cumbre Vieja volcano. In the novel’s scenario, the collapse would throw an enormous mass of earth into the Atlantic and raise a tsunami. Dragon Fire troops are sent to stop the attack before the consequences leave the island. The published description is careful to call that collapse the novel’s scenario. This preview repeats that caution. It is fiction about a plan, not a forecast. Continue on Operation Dragon Claw.

Trump’s Brass Escalator continues Sandstorm. The president, the vice-president, Special Forces leader Walker, and his female sergeant are in New York to present evidence gathered in Iran to the United Nations. Before they can, robbers attack Trump Tower and take the vice-president hostage. Walker’s team has to break off the larger mission to stop the robbery and save a life at the center of government. Continue on Trump’s Brass Escalator.

The Scorpion Sting begins after Eagle’s Talon. Saddam Hussein sends another group into the United States to kill the Iraqi operative American forces captured, and to kill soldiers involved in that arrest. Walker’s team has to find the new cell. The danger follows the soldiers home. The published description is a retaliation story, which is why the series page treats it as the continuation rather than as a second first chapter. Continue on The Scorpion Sting.
