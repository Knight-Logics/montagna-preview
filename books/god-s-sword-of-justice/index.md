## Nine hours at Indian Point: a nuclear reactor turned into a weapon

God’s Sword of Justice is a nuclear-terrorism thriller set at the Indian Point nuclear power plant in New York. Terrorists find a way into the control room, close the C-2 valve and stop the cooling water to the reactor. That starts a nine-hour window before meltdown, with a hundred-mile radius at risk of radioactive contamination for a hundred years. Walker’s elite soldiers are sent to stop them.

The book portfolio shows how long the attack was in the making. The story opens after September 11 and moves to Afghanistan in October 2001, where Colonel Mahathir Abdullah al-Shatti is chosen for a long mission against the United States; it follows him to Miami and a sleeper agent, Laaha al-Jabali, before the crisis on the Hudson. Captain Robert Walker and Colonel Bruce Leadbetter lead the response. The portfolio gives the novel’s length as 740 pages, copyright 2026.

### Is Indian Point a real nuclear power plant?

Yes. Indian Point was a real nuclear plant on the Hudson River north of New York City; its last reactor shut down in 2021. The attack in the novel is fiction.

### How long is God’s Sword of Justice?

740 pages, according to the book’s portfolio, which also gives its genre as a military and counterterrorism thriller and its copyright year as 2026.

### Is God’s Sword of Justice a standalone novel?

Yes. God’s Sword of Justice stands alone; no other book needs to be read first. It shares characters with other novels: Captain Robert Walker also appears across the author’s other thrillers and leads the [Scorpion Trilogy](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/series/scorpion/).
