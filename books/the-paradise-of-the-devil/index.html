THE STORY

A coup in Cuba. A buried secret. A nation on the brink.

A WORK OF FICTION

A Cuban colonel, disillusioned with the state of his country’s military, confronts Castro, kills him, and takes command of the island. He expects the Cuban people to support his takeover. Instead, a civilian army rises against his troops. As the struggle for control intensifies, a secret left over from the Cuban Missile Crisis draws the United States into a confrontation with consequences far beyond Cuba.

The Paradise of the Devil; A Cuban Colonel fed up with the condition of the military, confronts Castro. The Colonel kills Castro, takes command of Cuba, thinking the civilians of Cuba would support his takeover of the Cuban Island. The Colonel underestimated the Cuban people and a civilian army is organized and attack the Colonel's troops. Discover the secret Castro sat on since the Cuban missile crisis and what that secret causes the United States to respond.
