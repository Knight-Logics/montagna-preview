READER’S GUIDE

A female samurai in 1333 Japan and the curse that binds her to her sword

Birth of Wind by Stephen Francis Montagna is a female samurai historical fantasy (Wind Trilogy, Book 1). In a world that denied her a place, she became a legend.

Birth of Wind is the first novel of the Wind Trilogy, a historical fantasy that begins in Japan in 1333. Its heroine, the samurai called Wind, is placed in the middle of one of the most male-dominated eras in history. Her determination, skill and disciplined fighting carry her to the end, and at her death the shogun places a curse on her sword that binds the warrior and the blade together for eternity.

That curse is the thread of the whole trilogy. The editorial review published with the novel, by Dan Holloway for Pacific Book Review, notes that it opens with a prologue in the creation myth of Izanagi and Izanami before moving into recorded history. The book portfolio names its themes as defiance against tradition, identity and self-worth, honor beyond recognition, sacrifice, strength through adversity, and war and its consequences.

Questions about Birth of Wind

What is Birth of Wind about?

In Japan in 1333, a female samurai fights for her place in a society dominated by men. Her discipline, determination, and skill carry her through a life of conflict. When her story reaches its end, a curse placed on her sword by the shogun binds the warrior and the blade together for eternity. Birth of Wind begins a trilogy that carries her legacy across centuries.

What is a female samurai called, and were there real ones?

Women of samurai families who trained for battle are known as onna-bugeisha or onna-musha. The best known is Tomoe Gozen, who fought in the Genpei War of the 1180s. Wind is a fictional heroine set in 1333.

Is there a trailer for Birth of Wind?

Yes. The official trailer runs 2 minutes 59 seconds and was published on YouTube on 10 August 2026. A separate 71-second film introduces the whole Wind Trilogy in the screening room.

Where does Birth of Wind fit in the Wind Trilogy?

It is book 1 of 3. The reading order is Birth of Wind, then The Wind’s Breathe, then Night Wind.
