The accidental discovery of secret military papers exposes a deadly threat. The Iraqi military has developed a designer biological weapon. American intelligence quickly learns that the pathogen will mutate and destroy all life on the planet if it is unleashed. Heading an elite rapid response team, Captain Robert Walker leads his soldiers into the heart of the Iraq desert to track down a young woman rumored to know of the weapon's location. Time is running out as Captain Walker and his squad race to find the biological agent before its madman creator can use it to destroy the world.

Daughter of the Scorpion turns on secret military papers that expose an Iraqi biological weapons program. American intelligence learns the pathogen could mutate and threaten life on a global scale if it is released. Captain Robert Walker leads a rapid response team into the Iraqi desert to find a young woman believed to know where the weapon is hidden. The clock in the published description is that search, not a courtroom drama and not a laboratory procedural.

Five further novels sit one link away from this page. Each note below is the published situation of that book, not a new plot. Follow the one that matches the search you actually typed.

Code Cold Fire Alert is set in the plants the United States builds to destroy its own chemical and biological stockpiles. Crews are sealed inside for long shifts and ordered to stay if a spill cannot be contained. A spill happens. Doctors try to leave after disconnecting a nuclear sterilization device. A lone colonel goes into the containment area to restore its power, knowing he may have to trigger the final safeguard by hand. Continue on Code Cold Fire Alert.

Massive Vinegar Joe Platform is a Pacific confrontation. China seeks United Nations approval for an attack on North Korea, presented as an effort to eliminate nuclear weapons. Behind that promise is a broader deception involving Hong Kong and Taiwan. In the novel’s scenario the United States has no nearby base, and it turns to a massive floating platform. The published description is about pretext, alliance, and a machine built because the map will not cooperate. Continue on Massive Vinegar Joe Platform.

Eagle’s Talon is the opening novel of the Scorpion Trilogy. In the published story, Saddam Hussein sends a terrorist group to assassinate the American president in an effort to end sanctions. Robert Walker and his Special Forces troops are sent to stop the attack, and the mission then takes them into Iraq to capture the terrorist leader and bring him to the United States for trial. Continue on Eagle’s Talon.

Night Wind closes the trilogy’s published arc on this site. The sword is discovered by the FBI. The agent who takes responsibility brings it to the United States and learns it is not an ordinary weapon. Wind remains bound to protect the person who possesses it. That duty, not a new origin story, is what the third novel puts in a new country. Continue on Night Wind.

The Vector is the preview’s plain example of a geopolitical thriller. Iran answers a war with a covert biological attack on the United States. Eight volunteers are fitted with false teeth that hold a deadly agent and are sent through Mexico toward eight American cities. Their orders are to release the agent and spread the infection before it kills them. The weapon in the published description is a person, not a missile. Continue on The Vector.
