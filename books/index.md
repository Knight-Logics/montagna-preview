## Every Stephen Francis Montagna book, by series and by subject

The library holds all 21 current novels. Six belong to the two named trilogies: the Wind Trilogy—[Birth of Wind](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/birth-of-wind/), [The Wind’s Breathe](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-wind-s-breathe/) and [Night Wind](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/night-wind/)—and the Scorpion Trilogy—[Eagle’s Talon](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/eagle-s-talon/), [The Scorpion Sting](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-scorpion-sting/) and [Daughter of the Scorpion](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/daughter-of-the-scorpion/). The other fifteen stand alone, though several share characters.

## Questions and answers

### How many books are in the Stephen Francis Montagna library?

Twenty-one current novels: three in the Wind Trilogy, three in the Scorpion Trilogy and fifteen standalone books.

### Can Stephen Francis Montagna’s books be read in any order?

Yes, except the two trilogies, which read best in order, and [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/), which continues [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/).

### Which books feature Robert Walker?

Robert Walker leads the Scorpion Trilogy and also appears, at different ranks, in [The Paradise of the Devil](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/), [Bio-Level Three](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/bio-level-three/), [Sandstorm](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/sandstorm/), [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/), [The Vulture Will Lead You to Death](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-vulture-will-lead-you-to-death/) and [God’s Sword of Justice](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/god-s-sword-of-justice/).
