## Six centuries later, the cursed sword wakes in modern Japan

The Wind’s Breathe is the second novel of the Wind Trilogy. After the fantastical finale of [Birth of Wind](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/birth-of-wind/) in 1333, Wind’s cursed sword is discovered in Japan in 1996. Its new owner—her commander, in the author’s words—uses her samurai skills against his competition in the Japanese construction industry.

The editorial review published with the novel, by Alexandra Pierce for Pacific Book Review, calls it more intense and more refined than the first book, deepening the saga’s themes of power, destiny and the pull of divine forces and human ambition. Read Birth of Wind first; [Night Wind](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/night-wind/) carries the sword on to the United States.

### Is the title The Wind’s Breathe or The Wind’s Breath?

The published title is The Wind’s Breathe, with an e. The review published with the novel prints it as “The Winds Breathe.”

### Do I need to read Birth of Wind first?

Yes. The second novel begins centuries after [Birth of Wind](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/birth-of-wind/), and the curse that ties Wind to the sword is set up there.
