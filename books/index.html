READER’S GUIDE

Every Stephen Francis Montagna book, by series and by subject

The complete catalog of Stephen Francis Montagna’s 21 current novels: six in the Wind and Scorpion trilogies and fifteen standalone thrillers and speculative novels.

The library holds all 21 current novels. Six belong to the two named trilogies: the Wind Trilogy—Birth of Wind, The Wind’s Breathe and Night Wind—and the Scorpion Trilogy—Eagle’s Talon, The Scorpion Sting and Daughter of the Scorpion. The other fifteen stand alone, though several share characters.

Questions about Stephen Francis Montagna’s books

How many books are in the Stephen Francis Montagna library?

Twenty-one current novels: three in the Wind Trilogy, three in the Scorpion Trilogy and fifteen standalone books.

Can Stephen Francis Montagna’s books be read in any order?

Yes, except the two trilogies, which read best in order, and Trump’s Brass Escalator, which continues Sandstorm.

Which books feature Robert Walker?

Robert Walker leads the Scorpion Trilogy and also appears, at different ranks, in The Paradise of the Devil, Bio-Level Three, Sandstorm, Trump’s Brass Escalator, The Vulture Will Lead You to Death and God’s Sword of Justice.

How do I search the library?

Use the filters at the top of the page, or add a search to the address: /books/?q=samurai finds the Wind Trilogy, and /books/?collection=scorpion shows the Scorpion books.
