READER’S GUIDE

An assassination plot, a capture mission in Iraq and the start of the Scorpion Trilogy

Eagle’s Talon by Stephen Francis Montagna is a special forces military thriller (Scorpion Trilogy, Book 1). An assassination plot. An elite team. A race into hostile territory.

Eagle’s Talon opens the Scorpion Trilogy. Iraqi President Saddam Hussein wants the sanctions choking his country lifted, so he sends a terrorist group set on killing the American President. Special Forces team leader Robert Walker and his troops are sent to stop the attack, and then into Iraq to capture the leader of the terrorists and bring him back to the United States to stand trial.

The editorial review published with the novel, by Caroline M. Reyes, sets it in the volatile aftermath of the Gulf War and Operation Desert Storm and recommends it to readers of military and political thrillers grounded in real-world events. Start the trilogy here: the capture mission is what the next novel, The Scorpion Sting, is about, and Daughter of the Scorpion completes it.

Questions about Eagle’s Talon

What is Eagle’s Talon about?

Iraqi President Saddam Hussein sends a terrorist group to assassinate the American President in an effort to end the sanctions on his country. Special Forces leader Robert Walker and his troops are tasked with stopping the attack. Their mission then takes them into Iraq, where they must capture the terrorist leader and bring him to the United States to stand trial. This is the opening story of the Scorpion Trilogy.

Is Eagle’s Talon the first book in the Scorpion Trilogy?

Yes. Eagle’s Talon is book one, and the author describes The Scorpion Sting as continuing where it ends. Daughter of the Scorpion completes the trilogy.
