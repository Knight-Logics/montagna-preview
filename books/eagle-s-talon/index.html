A WORK OF FICTION

Iraqi President Saddam Hussein sends a terrorist group to assassinate the American President in an effort to end the sanctions on his country. Special Forces leader Robert Walker and his troops are tasked with stopping the attack. Their mission then takes them into Iraq, where they must capture the terrorist leader and bring him to the United States to stand trial. This is the opening story of the Scorpion Trilogy.

Iraqi President Saddam Hussein wants to end the choking sanctions on his country, so he sends a terrorist group hell bent on killing the American President.
