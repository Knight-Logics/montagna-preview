## An assassination plot, a capture mission in Iraq and the start of the Scorpion Trilogy

Eagle’s Talon opens the Scorpion Trilogy. Iraqi President Saddam Hussein wants the sanctions choking his country lifted, so he sends a terrorist group set on killing the American President. Special Forces team leader Robert Walker and his troops are sent to stop the attack, and then into Iraq to capture the leader of the terrorists and bring him back to the United States to stand trial.

The editorial review published with the novel, by Caroline M. Reyes, sets it in the volatile aftermath of the Gulf War and Operation Desert Storm and recommends it to readers of military and political thrillers grounded in real-world events. Start the trilogy here: the capture mission is what the next novel, [The Scorpion Sting](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/the-scorpion-sting/), is about, and [Daughter of the Scorpion](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/daughter-of-the-scorpion/) completes it.

### Is Eagle’s Talon the first book in the Scorpion Trilogy?

Yes. Eagle’s Talon is book one, and the author describes [The Scorpion Sting](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/the-scorpion-sting/) as continuing where it ends. [Daughter of the Scorpion](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/daughter-of-the-scorpion/) completes the trilogy.
