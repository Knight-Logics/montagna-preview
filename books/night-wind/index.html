READER’S GUIDE

The sword crosses the Pacific with an FBI agent

Night Wind by Stephen Francis Montagna is a samurai fantasy thriller (Wind Trilogy, Book 3). The sword crosses an ocean. The warrior’s duty follows.

Night Wind is the third novel of the Wind Trilogy. Once again Wind’s sword is discovered, this time by the FBI. The agent in charge of it returns to the United States and quickly learns that the sword is special.

Wind is obligated to save the life of whoever possesses the blade, so she is always in the way of the danger aimed at him. The editorial review published with the novel, by Jon Carlisle for Pacific Book Review, describes a fusion of mythology, supernatural intrigue and modern-day suspense that intertwines ancient samurai legend with contemporary crime fiction.

Questions about Night Wind

What is Night Wind about?

Wind’s sword is discovered once again, this time by the FBI. The agent responsible for it brings the blade back to the United States and soon learns that it is far more than an ordinary weapon. Wind remains bound to protect the person who possesses it, bringing the warrior’s enduring obligation into the agent’s life. The third novel carries the Wind Trilogy across the Pacific into a new setting.

Is Night Wind the last book in the Wind Trilogy?

Yes. Night Wind is book three, after Birth of Wind and The Wind’s Breathe. The review published with it calls it the culminating volume.
