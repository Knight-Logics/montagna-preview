## La Palma, the Cumbre Vieja crack and a wave 75 meters high

Operation Dragon Claw joins a military thriller to a disaster novel. A group of terrorists invades the small island of La Palma in the Canary Islands to plant explosives along the crack in the side of the dormant Cumbre Vieja volcano. The aim is to drop more than 125 million tons of earth into the Atlantic Ocean and raise a mega-tsunami, a wave 75 meters high.

The Dragon Fire troops are dispatched to stop the attack. The scenario takes its premise from a real and much-argued scientific hypothesis about the volcano’s western flank, which is why the plot reads as plausible as well as cinematic. The editorial review published with the novel, by Amber Carlisle, describes a story that combines modern terrorism, elite special operations and a terrifying natural-disaster scenario.

### Could a La Palma landslide really cause a mega-tsunami?

The idea comes from a scientific hypothesis, first published in 2001, that a collapse of Cumbre Vieja’s western flank could send a tsunami across the Atlantic. Later studies have disputed its likelihood and scale. The terrorist plot in the novel is fiction.

### Is Operation Dragon Claw a standalone novel?

Yes. Operation Dragon Claw stands alone; no other book needs to be read first. It shares characters with other novels: the Dragon Fire troops also carry [Operation Dragon Fire](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/operation-dragon-fire/) and [Bio-Level Three](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/bio-level-three/).
