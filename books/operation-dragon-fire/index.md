Then the terrorist planned to move their operations to Washington where they intend to attack a baseball game between the Mets and the Washington Commanders. ## Three attacks as cover for a dirty bomb in Washington

Operation Dragon Fire is a counterterrorism military thriller. An Iraqi man let into America because he helped American troops in Iraq organizes a group of young men and plans four attacks: first the New York subway system, with Kennedy International Airport struck at the same time, and then, in Washington, a baseball game.

Those three attacks are cover. The real objective is a dirty nuclear weapon set off between the White House and the Capitol, and the specialized Dragon Fire troops are called on again to stop it at all costs—the same unit that goes into the Libyan desert in [Bio-Level Three](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/bio-level-three/) and to the Canary Islands in [Operation Dragon Claw](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/operation-dragon-claw/). The editorial review published with the novel recommends it to fans of Tom Clancy-style fiction.

## Questions and answers

### Who are the Dragon Fire troops?

A specialized American unit that recurs in three novels: Operation Dragon Fire, [Bio-Level Three](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/bio-level-three/) and [Operation Dragon Claw](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/operation-dragon-claw/). The author has not published a numbered order for the three, and each can be read on its own.

### Where is Operation Dragon Fire set?

New York—its subway system and Kennedy International Airport—and then Washington, D.C., where the final target lies between the White House and the Capitol.

### Is Operation Dragon Fire a standalone novel?

Yes. Operation Dragon Fire stands alone; no other book needs to be read first.
