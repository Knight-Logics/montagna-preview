THE STORY

The mission is over. The retaliation has only begun.

A WORK OF FICTION

The story continues after Eagle’s Talon. Saddam Hussein sends another terrorist group into the United States to kill the Iraqi operative captured by American forces, along with soldiers involved in his arrest. Walker and his Special Forces team must locate the new cell and stop its campaign of retaliation. The danger follows the soldiers home, turning the aftermath of one mission into the beginning of another.

Read the full published book description ＋

This novel continues where 'Eagle's Talon' ended. When another Iraqi terrorist group is sent to the United States by President Saddam Hussein to kill the Iraqi operative captured and brought to the United States to stand trail, along with killing some of the American troops who invaded Iraq to take the lead terrorist prisoner. Walker and his Special Forces troops search and find and then kill the terrorist cell working in the United States and end the threat aimed against the American soldiers.
