BOOK OVERVIEW

SOURCE PAGE 05

Trump’s Brass Escalator is a high-stakes military and political thriller that continues the events surrounding the Special Forces operation in Iran established in Sandstorm.

The novel opens with President Albert Cole reviewing the evidence recovered from the Iranian nuclear complex destroyed during the previous operation. The material provides the American government with critical evidence concerning Iran’s nuclear ambitions and the weapons-development program uncovered by the Special Forces. President Cole and his senior military and intelligence officials prepare to present that evidence before the United Nations.

At the center of the military side of the story is Lieutenant Robert Walker, an exceptionally aggressive and highly respected Special Forces leader, and Sergeant Dorothy Ramirez, his trusted partner and fellow soldier. Their unit returns from Iran battered and grieving after losing Sergeant Barbara Meyerhoff. The emotional aftermath of the Iranian operation gives the novel a human dimension beneath its military action.

SOURCE PAGE 06

Meanwhile, Vice President Mary Hirshfield travels to New York ahead of the United Nations meeting and takes up residence in the Trump International Tower and Hotel. Unknown to her security team, a criminal organization is preparing a massive robbery of the building.

The criminals are after a fortune in diamonds being held by diamond dealers inside the Tower. Their plan is not merely to steal the diamonds. They intend to seize hostages, exploit the building’s security systems, manipulate the emergency response, and use violence to create an escape route.

Their carefully constructed operation changes completely when they discover that the Vice President of the United States is inside the building.

What begins as a robbery becomes an unprecedented national-security crisis.

The attackers seize the Tower, trap hundreds of civilians behind the building’s fire doors, and take the Vice President hostage. The discovery transforms the criminals’ bargaining position and forces Washington to confront an impossible question: how can the Vice President be rescued without sacrificing the lives of the other hostages?

SOURCE PAGE 07

The FBI mobilizes its Hostage Rescue Units, while President Cole demands that every possible measure be taken to save the Vice President. At the same time, General John White argues that his specialized military forces may be better suited to the increasingly dangerous situation.

When the initial FBI assault ends disastrously and the terrorists demonstrate that they possess far greater firepower than expected, the crisis escalates dramatically.

The President ultimately turns to Lieutenant Walker and Sergeant Ramirez—the two Special Forces soldiers who happen to be trapped inside the Tower.

Walker is given one overriding mission:

Reach the Vice President. Free her. Keep her alive.

From that point forward, Trump’s Brass Escalator becomes a race against time through a skyscraper transformed into a battlefield.

SOURCE PAGE 09

President Albert Cole is preparing to address the United Nations with evidence recovered from the Iranian nuclear facility destroyed during the events of Sandstorm. The evidence demonstrates the seriousness of the Iranian weapons program and provides the United States with material it intends to present before the international community. General John White and the MNRRF have recovered extensive documentation, photographs, equipment, and weapons-related evidence from the Iranian operation.

The political mission brings together the President, Vice President Mary Hirshfield, intelligence officials, military commanders, and members of the Special Forces team responsible for the Iranian operation.

Among them is Lieutenant Robert Walker.

Walker is not a conventional military officer. He is blunt, aggressive, unconventional, and intensely loyal to the soldiers under his command. His relationship with Sergeant Dorothy Ramirez provides another layer to his character. Ramirez is not simply Walker’s partner; she is an accomplished soldier who understands his temperament, challenges him when necessary, and stands beside him in combat.

At its heart, Trump’s Brass Escalator is the story of an international military crisis colliding with a domestic hostage situation.

The novel moves from the evidence recovered during a covert operation in Iran to the political arena of the United Nations, then suddenly shifts into a large-scale urban crisis inside one of New York City’s most prominent buildings.

The central question becomes:

Can the Vice President be rescued before the terrorists turn their robbery into a massacre?

The answer depends not only on the resources of the United States government, but on two soldiers already trapped inside the building.

Trump’s Brass Escalator takes the military and international conflict established in Sandstorm and brings it directly into the heart of New York City.

What begins as a sophisticated diamond robbery develops into a national-security emergency when the criminals discover that the Vice President of the United States is inside the building.

With the FBI struggling to overcome unexpectedly heavy resistance, and the military unable to immediately deploy its forces, Lieutenant Robert Walker and Sergeant Dorothy Ramirez find themselves inside the besieged Tower—and become America's best chance of saving the Vice President.

The result is a sprawling action thriller that moves from the Pentagon and the international arena to a skyscraper transformed into a battlefield.
