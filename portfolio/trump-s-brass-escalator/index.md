## Inside the Trump’s Brass Escalator portfolio

The portfolio is Stephen Francis Montagna’s own presentation of [Trump’s Brass Escalator](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/trump-s-brass-escalator/): what the story is, who carries it and what it is about. It follows the evidence from a covert operation in Iran to the United Nations and then into the hostage crisis, and asks whether the Vice President can be rescued before the robbery becomes a massacre.

- President Albert Cole
- Vice President Mary Hirshfield
- Lieutenant Robert Walker
- FBI Director Hedemann

### Themes of Trump’s Brass Escalator

- Duty against personal loyalty
- Leadership under pressure
- The cost of war
- Courage under extreme circumstances
- Political power and responsibility
- Greed and violence
- Sacrifice
- Brotherhood and team loyalty
