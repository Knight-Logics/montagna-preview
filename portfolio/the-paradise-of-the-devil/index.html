THE SECRET CASTRO LEFT BEHIND

SOURCE PAGE 19

The novel's second major plot engine emerges from the history surrounding the Cuban Missile Crisis.

The manuscript establishes that the world believed the Soviet nuclear weapons had been removed from Cuba after the 1962 crisis.

But Castro had kept a secret.

The manuscript explicitly states that Castro concealed information from the United States, a secret that would eventually return to threaten America.

Decades later, Alvarez discovers that the secret is far more dangerous than anyone realizes.

Hidden facilities contain nuclear weapons and missile systems.

This discovery changes Alvarez's strategy completely.

The Cuban civilian rebellion is no longer his only problem.

He now possesses something capable of forcing the United States to pay attention.

SOURCE PAGE 20

ALVAREZ'S DEADLIEST DECISION

SOURCE PAGE 21

Instead of surrendering or negotiating, Alvarez decides to use the hidden missiles as his ultimate weapon.

He believes the United States will be forced to respond to the threat.

The missiles become his final bargaining chip—and eventually

something much darker.

The discovery of documents inside the hidden bunker reveals that Castro had planned military actions in the Middle East as diversions while missiles were moved into launch positions. The plans include operations involving Israel, Lebanon, Syria and Jordan.

The revelation expands the novel from a Cuban civil conflict into

an international crisis.

Cuba's internal revolution now threatens to become a global catastrophe.

The stakes become enormous.

He is now provoking:

the United States

UNIQUE STORY HOOK

A Cuban colonel assassinates Fidel Castro believing he can save his country—only to discover that the people he intended to liberate would rather fight him than accept another military ruler. When he uncovers a secret nuclear arsenal hidden in Cuba since the Cuban Missile Crisis, his failed revolution transforms into a race against the United States to prevent him from taking the entire world to the brink of nuclear war.

SUBMISSION SYNOPSIS

SOURCE PAGE 55

The Paradise of the Devil follows Colonel Carlos Rafael Hernandez Alvarez, a Cuban military officer who has grown increasingly convinced that Fidel Castro's leadership has destroyed Cuba.

Alvarez secretly gathers military officers and political allies who agree that Castro must be removed. Their plan culminates in Alvarez confronting Castro personally. When Castro attempts to recruit Alvarez into his personal staff, Alvarez instead draws a pistol and takes control of the confrontation.

Castro is killed.

Alvarez becomes President.

Initially, his supporters believe Cuba has finally been liberated. But Alvarez has underestimated the Cuban population. Civilian resistance rapidly develops into an organized army, capturing territory and attacking government military installations. Soldiers begin abandoning their posts and, in some cases, joining the civilian resistance.

The situation deteriorates into a nationwide civil war.

SOURCE PAGE 56

Alvarez responds with military force, increasingly behaving like the dictator he claimed to overthrow. Maria Ibarra, his lover and former supporter, becomes increasingly horrified by his transformation. She warns him that he is becoming like Castro.

Then Alvarez discovers a secret that changes the entire conflict.

Hidden in Cuba are nuclear weapons connected to the secret military infrastructure left over from the Cold War. Castro had concealed the existence of this capability from the United States.

Alvarez decides to use the missiles as leverage against America.

As intelligence concerning the weapons reaches Washington, the United States begins preparing a military intervention. American forces converge on Cuba while Corporal Robert Walker leads a dangerous ground assault toward the hidden missile installation.

Inside the bunker, Alvarez initiates the missile launch sequence.

IN THE LIBRARY

Turning the pages of The Paradise of the Devil

The portfolio of The Paradise of the Devil is the set of pages in the reader above. This guide stays under the reader so the book can still be searched, shelved, and connected to the rest of the library.

The Paradise of the Devil begins with a Cuban colonel who has lost faith in the condition of the military. He confronts Castro, kills him, and takes command of the island, expecting the people to support the takeover. A civilian army rises against his troops instead. Under that civil war sits a secret left from the Cuban Missile Crisis, and the secret pulls the United States into a confrontation that does not stay in Cuba.

Five further novels sit one link away from this page. Each note below is the published situation of that book, not a new plot. Follow the one that matches the search you actually typed.

Beyond Yesterday begins after a nuclear exchange has destroyed most of the world’s population. Survivors learn how to reach into the past. Attempts to replenish humanity with reproductive material fail. They turn to taking people from earlier times, including the pilots of five aircraft that disappeared in the Bermuda Triangle. Each journey leaves consequences in the Atlantic. The mysteries of those disappearances become part of a desperate effort to keep a human future. Continue on Beyond Yesterday.

Sandstorm sends American special operations soldiers into Iran. A rogue Russian admiral has sold Iran nuclear-tipped missiles launched from submarines. The team has to destroy the missiles and the submarines, and it has to bring back evidence of the transfer, evidence that could show how Iran acquired the means to build and launch a nuclear threat against the United States. The published description is a raid plus a proof. Continue on Sandstorm.

Daughter of the Scorpion turns on secret military papers that expose an Iraqi biological weapons program. American intelligence learns the pathogen could mutate and threaten life on a global scale if it is released. Captain Robert Walker leads a rapid response team into the Iraqi desert to find a young woman believed to know where the weapon is hidden. The clock in the published description is that search, not a courtroom drama and not a laboratory procedural. Continue on Daughter of the Scorpion.

The Wind’s Breathe is the second novel. Centuries after Birth of Wind, the cursed sword is found in Japan in 1996. Its new owner turns Wind’s samurai abilities against rivals in the construction industry. The published description is that specific: not a return to the fourteenth-century war, but the same binding put to work in a modern commercial fight. The title on the author’s materials uses this spelling, and so does this preview. Continue on The Wind’s Breathe.

The Vulture Will Lead You to Death follows terrorists who enter the United States from Cuba with a plan against the Port of Miami. They know a supertanker is docked there. They do not know that a ship carrying military supplies is stationed nearby, and that its presence could magnify the blast. The published description stops on that unawareness. The novel is the working out of an attack whose second weapon is the port itself. Continue on The Vulture Will Lead You to Death.
