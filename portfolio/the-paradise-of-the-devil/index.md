## Inside The Paradise of the Devil portfolio

The portfolio is Stephen Francis Montagna’s own presentation of [The Paradise of the Devil](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/): what the story is, who carries it and what it is about. The portfolio sets the coup in five acts, from the seed of revolution to the confrontation with the United States, and gives the novel’s short pitch.
