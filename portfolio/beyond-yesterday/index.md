## Inside the Beyond Yesterday portfolio

The portfolio is Stephen Francis Montagna’s own presentation of [Beyond Yesterday](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/beyond-yesterday/): what the story is, who carries it and what it is about. It lists the five Flight 19 aircraft by their markings (FT-28, FT-36, FT-81, FT-3 and FT-117), explains the hurricanes each mission causes, and reads the title as yesterday, today and tomorrow.
