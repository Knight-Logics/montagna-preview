# About Stephen Francis Montagna — Novelist Biography

> Stephen Francis Montagna is a Brooklyn-born American novelist, drawn to Florida and the Keys, who has written military thrillers, espionage and historical fantasy since 1996.

Biography of novelist Stephen Francis Montagna: born in Brooklyn, drawn to the Florida Keys, writing since 1996—military thrillers, espionage and fantasy.

## Stephen Francis Montagna at a glance

- Name: Stephen Francis Montagna (also credited as Stephen F. Montagna)
- Born: Brooklyn, New York, into a hard-working Italian family
- Home waters: Florida and the Keys—fishing, boating and time on the water
- Writing since: 1996
- Books: 21 current novels, including the [Wind Trilogy](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/series/wind/) and the [Scorpion Trilogy](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/series/scorpion/)
- Genres: military and special-forces thrillers, counterterrorism, espionage, speculative fiction, samurai historical fantasy
- Mentor he credits: the artist Mary Motches Montagna

## Questions and answers

### Where is Stephen Francis Montagna from?

He was born in Brooklyn, New York. His life later brought him to Florida and the Keys.

### When did Stephen Francis Montagna start writing?

He began his writing career in 1996.

### What has Stephen Francis Montagna said about writing?

“My writing allows me to be whoever I am writing about.” And of writing itself: “It is my passion, my mistress, lover, monster, and dream.”
