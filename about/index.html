STEPHEN FRANCIS MONTAGNA

THE LIFE BEHIND THE PAGES

From Brooklyn
to worlds beyond.

Stephen Francis Montagna began his writing career in 1996. His stories span military thrillers, espionage, historical fantasy, and speculative fiction.

Born in Brooklyn, New York, he describes an upbringing shaped by a hard-working Italian family. His life later brought him to Florida and the Keys, where fishing, boating, and time on the water offer a counterpoint to the high stakes of his fiction.

In the Wind Trilogy, a female samurai and her sword carry a story across centuries. In the Scorpion Trilogy, Captain Robert Walker confronts threats that place both his team and the wider world at risk. Elsewhere in the collection, scientific discoveries, political crises, and the future itself become the starting points for new stories.

Montagna credits artist Mary Motches Montagna as a formative mentor in his writing life—his dictionary, his encouragement, and a driving force behind his early novels.

“My writing allows me to be whoever I am writing about.”

READER’S GUIDE

Stephen Francis Montagna at a glance

Stephen Francis Montagna is a Brooklyn-born American novelist, drawn to Florida and the Keys, who has written military thrillers, espionage and historical fantasy since 1996.

  • Name: Stephen Francis Montagna (also credited as Stephen F. Montagna)
  • Born: Brooklyn, New York, into a hard-working Italian family
  • Home waters: Florida and the Keys—fishing, boating and time on the water
  • Writing since: 1996
  • Books: 21 current novels, including the Wind Trilogy and the Scorpion Trilogy
  • Genres: military and special-forces thrillers, counterterrorism, espionage, speculative fiction, samurai historical fantasy
  • Mentor he credits: the artist Mary Motches Montagna
