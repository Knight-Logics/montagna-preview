Four films from two trilogies

Four films for Stephen Francis Montagna’s books: trailers for Birth of Wind, Eagle’s Talon and Daughter of the Scorpion, and a 71-second film for the Wind Trilogy.

  • Birth of Wind — official trailer, 2 minutes 59 seconds, published 10 August 2026
  • Eagle’s Talon — trailer, 2 minutes 46 seconds, published 13 August 2026
  • Daughter of the Scorpion — trailer, 3 minutes 12 seconds, published 13 August 2026

The three book trailers are on YouTube and play here in a privacy-enhanced player that loads only when you press play. The Wind Trilogy film plays directly from this site.

Questions about the book trailers

Which Stephen Francis Montagna books have trailers?

Birth of Wind, Eagle’s Talon and Daughter of the Scorpion, plus a film for the whole Wind Trilogy.

Where else can I watch the trailers?

On YouTube, and on each novel’s own page, where the trailer button sits beside the book.
