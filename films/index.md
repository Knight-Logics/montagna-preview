## Questions and answers

### Which Stephen Francis Montagna books have trailers?

The six Wind and Scorpion novels, plus [The Vector](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/the-vector/) and [Operation Glass Slipper](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/books/operation-glass-slipper/). There is also a film for the whole [Wind Trilogy](https://stephenfmontagnabooks.com/series/wind/).
