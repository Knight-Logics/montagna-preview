# New Release Thriller Books by Stephen Francis Montagna

> The seven newest Stephen Francis Montagna novels: The Paradise of the Devil, The Vulture Will Lead You to Death, The Vector, The Warning of Ramses, IFF, God’s Sword of Justice and Operation Glass Slipper.

Seven new thriller books by Stephen Francis Montagna: a coup in Cuba, a Miami seaport attack, Iran’s human bioweapons, a Nile water war and a stolen F-18.

## Seven new thrillers, seven different threats

- [The Paradise of the Devil](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/) — a colonel kills Castro and Cuba rises against him
- [The Vulture Will Lead You to Death](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-vulture-will-lead-you-to-death/) — terrorists from Cuba target a supertanker in the Port of Miami
- [The Vector](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-vector/) — after October 7, Iran sends eight human carriers toward eight American cities
- [The Warning of Ramses](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-warning-of-ramses/) — Ethiopia dams the Blue Nile and Egypt goes to war
- [IFF: Identify Friend or Foe](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/iff-identify-friend-or-foe/) — a stolen F-18 turns the fleet’s friend-or-foe system against a carrier
- [God’s Sword of Justice](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/god-s-sword-of-justice/) — terrorists take the Indian Point reactor control room
- [Operation Glass Slipper](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/operation-glass-slipper/) — a carbon-eating microbe becomes a terrorist’s bioweapon

## Questions and answers

### Which new release should I start with?

For Cuba and Cold War history, [The Paradise of the Devil](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-paradise-of-the-devil/); for espionage, [The Vector](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/the-vector/); for naval and air action, [IFF: Identify Friend or Foe](https://knight-logics.github.io/montagna-preview/books/iff-identify-friend-or-foe/). None of the seven needs another book first.
