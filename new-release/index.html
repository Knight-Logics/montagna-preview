The Paradise of the Devil

A coup in Cuba. A buried secret. A nation on the brink.

A Cuban colonel, disillusioned with the state of his country’s military, confronts Castro, kills him, and takes command of the island. He expects the Cuban people to support his takeover. Instead, a civilian army rises against his troops.
