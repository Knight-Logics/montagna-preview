READER’S GUIDE

Seven new thrillers, seven different threats

The seven newest Stephen Francis Montagna novels: The Paradise of the Devil, The Vulture Will Lead You to Death, The Vector, The Warning of Ramses, IFF, God’s Sword of Justice and Operation Glass Slipper.

  • The Paradise of the Devil — a colonel kills Castro and Cuba rises against him
  • The Vulture Will Lead You to Death — terrorists from Cuba target a supertanker in the Port of Miami
  • The Vector — after October 7, Iran sends eight human carriers toward eight American cities
  • The Warning of Ramses — Ethiopia dams the Blue Nile and Egypt goes to war
  • IFF: Identify Friend or Foe — a stolen F-18 turns the fleet’s friend-or-foe system against a carrier
  • God’s Sword of Justice — terrorists take the Indian Point reactor control room
  • Operation Glass Slipper — a carbon-eating microbe becomes a terrorist’s bioweapon

Questions about the new releases

Which new release should I start with?

For Cuba and Cold War history, The Paradise of the Devil; for espionage, The Vector; for naval and air action, IFF: Identify Friend or Foe. None of the seven needs another book first.
